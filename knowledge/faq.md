# Frequently Asked Questions: Juan Andrés Bernuy Navarrete

### What does Juan do?
He works where operations, systems and data meet. At Bullboat he makes sure the business runs on reliable information: he migrated the company's data from Sage to Cin7, integrated the systems with warehouse partners in the Netherlands and the UK, automated stock reporting and built Power BI dashboards. He is now leading the company's move to Microsoft Azure.

### How does he describe his way of working?
Tracing data from source to report: validating records after a migration, reconciling reports against the systems they come from, and fixing issues where they start rather than where they show up. He translates operational needs into system requirements.

### What makes his background different?
He started as a telecommunications network technician (fibre and RJ45 cabling), moved into supply chain at Zamora Company and then into systems and data at Bullboat, while studying Business Administration and Data Science at ESIC. He combines an operations view with hands-on technical work.

### What is his experience with AI?
